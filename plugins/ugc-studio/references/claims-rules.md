# Claims rules

Every statement about the product in a script must come from the product's own listing:
attributes, description, or text visible in its official photos. Nothing else.

## Build the claims list before writing ideas

From the product sheet, write two lists in the product folder (`ficha.md`):

- **Allowed:** each claim with its source (attribute name, description sentence, or photo number).
- **Forbidden:** claims a viewer might expect but the listing does not support, plus anything in
  the categories below.

## Always forbidden

- Health, medical or body-result promises (cures, heals, treats, "protege la columna", "mejora la
  circulación", weight loss, results guaranteed), even if a listing photo says it.
- Absolute claims the listing does not state: "el mejor", "el único", "para todos", "nunca se
  rompe", load or durability numbers that are not in the listing.
- Prices, discounts, stock or shipping promises in the dialogue (they change; the ad handles them).
- Things the listing says are not included (for example "tuercas o placas no incluidas").
- Comparisons with named competitors.

## When in doubt

Use the softer version the listing supports ("se adapta a la mayoría de los sommiers" instead of
"a cualquier sommier"), or leave the claim out. A forbidden claim blocks the idea; fix the script
before showing it to the user.

## Stock

If the listing is paused or has no stock, tell the user before spending credits on a video.
