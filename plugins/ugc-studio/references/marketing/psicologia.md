# Psychology for e-commerce creatives

Used by `crear-creativos` step 3 when choosing angles (and by video ideas when useful). Short
extract of the marketing-psychology skill of Corey Haines' marketingskills (MIT), kept to what a
Meta creative for an online store can use honestly. A principle explains why a buyer reacts; it
never replaces an allowed claim. The studio's rules win: nothing here permits a statement that is
not in `ficha.md`'s allowed list, and a principle that needs a forbidden claim is dropped.

## Filter every principle through this

- No fake scarcity or urgency: no "últimas unidades", countdowns or "solo hoy" unless the user
  gives a real, current offer; stock and deadlines change (`claims-rules.md`).
- No invented numbers: no "miles de clientes", ratings, percentages or "el más vendido" that are
  not in the listing.
- No prices, discounts or shipping promises in the image or the dialogue unless
  `claims-rules.md` and `meta-ads.md` allow it; the ad handles current prices.
- No wording that points at the reader's body, health or personal attributes (`meta-ads.md`).
- A principle is a reason for a concept's angle, not a claim to print.

## Principles

### 1. Social proof
- **What:** people take their cue from what others like them chose or said.
- **Use:** a real buyer's words, verbatim and permitted, as the headline or a card
  (`formatos-meta.md`, review quotes); a real sales fact ("Lo más comprado de la categoría" only
  if the platform shows it and the user confirms). Angle: prueba social.
- **Filter:** no fabricated reviews, stars, counts or attributions; a synthetic actor is not a
  buyer testimonial.

### 2. Loss aversion
- **What:** avoiding a loss weighs more than getting an equal gain.
- **Use:** make the cost of the current situation concrete in the buyer's words (the thing that
  keeps happening: the cable on the floor, the pillow that goes flat) before the product
  appears. Angle: dolor.
- **Filter:** the loss is the current annoyance, never a deadline or a vanishing stock; no
  fear about health or safety the listing does not support.

### 3. Regret aversion (anxiety)
- **What:** people postpone a purchase when they fear choosing wrong.
- **Use:** answer the doubt that blocks the click: size, fit, compatibility, what is included,
  how to return. Take it from `Objeciones` and the Ansiedad force of `ficha.md`. Angle:
  objeción; formats: FAQ card, callout.
- **Filter:** the answer must be an allowed claim ("se adapta a la mayoría de los sommiers"), not
  an absolute; mention a guarantee or return only if the listing states it.

### 4. Framing
- **What:** the same fact lands differently by how it is put.
- **Use:** say what the buyer gets or keeps instead of what the product has ("tu escritorio
  ordenado" over "organizador de 4 compartimentos"); frame by use per day or per occasion when
  the listing supports it.
- **Filter:** reframing never stretches a fact; if the listing says "hasta 5 kg", the frame
  cannot say "aguanta todo".

### 5. Contrast and anchoring
- **What:** a thing is judged against what sits next to it.
- **Use:** contrast the usual way with this one on the same task (`comparativa`, "Sin / Con"),
  or an object next to the product to show size. Angle: comparación.
- **Filter:** compare with "lo de siempre" or the category, never a named competitor; no
  crossed-out reference prices or "antes/ahora" amounts.

### 6. Mental accounting
- **What:** people sort money and value into mental boxes, so the same spend feels different by
  the box it goes in.
- **Use:** place the product in the right box in words, not numbers: a gift ("para quien lo
  tiene todo"), a small daily treat, an upgrade of something used every day. Angle:
  regalo/ocasión, deseo.
- **Filter:** no price per day or per month, no installment math in the image; those are the ad's
  current-price job.

### 7. Endowment and imagined ownership
- **What:** people value what they already feel is theirs.
- **Use:** show the product in the buyer's own setting and moment (person using it, home scene,
  unboxing look) so they picture owning it; a close-up of texture or material for touch.
  Angle: deseo/aspiración, cómo se usa.
- **Filter:** the scene shows what the box really contains; no accessories that are not included.

### 8. Pratfall (honest small flaw)
- **What:** admitting a small limit makes the rest more believable.
- **Use:** state an honest limit the listing already has ("no incluye las placas") and turn it
  into clarity, not apology. Angle: objeción.
- **Filter:** only limits the listing states; never a made-up weakness for effect, and never one
  that pushes the buyer to a health or safety worry.

## Choosing

Pick the principle from the evidence, not the other way round: a buyer question suggests regret
aversion, an Empuje force suggests loss aversion, an Atracción force suggests endowment or framing. Name
the principle in the concept's "why it differs" only when it explains the angle.
